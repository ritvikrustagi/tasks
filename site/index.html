A FEW STEPS TO GET STARTED

Make room for
your new assistant.

This build is for Macs with an M-series chip. Intel Mac and Windows downloads aren't available yet.

Read the setup guide ↗

  1. Download and unzip.

    Download the ZIP above, then double-click it in Finder to extract AI Browser.app.

  2. Move it to Applications.

    Drag AI Browser.app into your Applications folder and open it. This alpha isn't notarized; see Apple's guidance if macOS can't verify the app.

  3. Connect your assistant.

    Install and sign in to Claude Code or Codex, then open Assistant in the browser toolbar and choose your provider. Turn Agent Mode on to let it work with your tabs.

An early build, ready to explore. AI Browser is built on BrowserOS. Your own provider account and its usage limits apply.
